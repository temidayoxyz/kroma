MANUFACTURING DISCIPLINE

The Four Pillars of KROMA Engineering

No surface-mount short-cuts in critical audio paths. Every unit is conceived as an heirloom acoustic instrument.

01 // TOPOLOGY

Discrete JFET Signal Paths

Every operational amplifier block is constructed from discrete matched transistor pairs rather than generic integrated circuit chips. This delivers unmatched dynamic breathing room and headroom.

02 // ALLOY

CNC Billet 6061-T6 Chassis

Each chassis is milled from an 18kg solid block of aircraft-grade aluminum to eliminate mechanical microphonics, EMI interference, and thermal inconsistency.

03 // POWER

Toroidal Linear Supplies

We refuse high-frequency switching power supplies. Dual oversized toroidal transformers with triple-filtered discrete regulation guarantee an ultra-quiet noise floor below -135dBu.

04 // CALIBRATION

168-Hour Burn-In Stress Test

Every completed instrument is heat-cycled and acoustically swept in our Zurich test chamber for seven consecutive days before signing the calibration certificate.
